## Visual Inspection

Record approved prototype/UI revisions, fixture/state/viewport, screenshot actually viewed, comparison findings and resolutions. Image capture alone is not inspection or approval.

## Browser/Device Evidence and Access Limitations

Record browser/version or physical device/OS/runtime, proven access, app identity and scenarios inspected. Separate Android/iOS evidence and browser/component results; explain access limitations.

## Regression and Review Disposition

Link review finding IDs, severity, correction revision and regression evidence. Block verification on unresolved mandatory failures; a changed golden image requires design approval.

## Pending Checks and Human Review Scenarios

For each pending item give setup/reset steps, actions, expected outcomes, owner and required evidence. Human native review may supply missing UI evidence, not erase code-test failures.

## Acceptance Record

Keep separate dated entries by mode and revision. Record declared scope, actual human decision, evidence and unresolved conditions. Record rejection/pending honestly; no agent-authored acceptance. Platform all-mock decisions are summarized in the delivery catalog.

Do not infer acceptance from tests, commits or agent assertions; distinguish code checks, visual inspection and native checks.
